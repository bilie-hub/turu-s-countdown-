Hands and fingers

  • Right hand: thumb on the A just above middle C. Almost everything sits between that A and the E above it.
  • Hook E D♯ D: fingers 5 4 3. D♯ is the black key between D and E. It comes three times, over A, G♯ and G.
  • Answers: A C A (bar 2), A C (bar 4), A C B-C B (bars 6–7), then C A.
  • Left hand: one bass note per two bars, sliding down A → G♯ → G and back to A. Hold it with the pedal.

How sure is this

  • Solid: every note here is the loudest pitch at its moment in your recording, and the rhythm sits on a 140 BPM grid within about 10 ms.
  • Slightly less sure: the quick C between the two B's at the end of bar 6, and whether the G♯ and G on beat 1 of bars 3 and 5 are in your right hand or doubled from the left.
  • Your recording restarts the loop at the end with a full A-minor chord (A C E) in the left hand. Use that on the repeat if you like the fuller sound.
